Course

Starts at LI1 beside the index fingernail, runs between the first two metacarpals (LI4) to the wrist (LI5), up the thumb side of the forearm to the outer end of the elbow crease (LI11), up the outer upper arm to the front of the shoulder (LI15) and over it to LI16. It meets the other yang channels at GV14 below the seventh neck vertebra, then turns forward to ST12 above the collarbone, where a branch goes inward to the lung and the large intestine. The surface branch climbs the neck (LI17, LI18), crosses the cheek into the lower gums, circles the upper lip and crosses the midline at GV26, ending at LI20 beside the opposite nostril.
